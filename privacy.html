Die App Pathrova

Pathrova erhebt, speichert und überträgt keine personenbezogenen Daten. Die App baut keine Internetverbindung auf, braucht kein Konto, zeigt keine Werbung und nutzt keine Analyse- oder Absturzdienste.

Privacy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. The controller is the provider named in the legal notice.

The Pathrova app

Pathrova does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. The app makes no internet connections and has no account, no ads and no analytics or crash services.

  • Pathrova only reads the folder you choose. It changes file or folder names only when you explicitly confirm renames in the preview.
  • Settings and the undo logs of renames (old and new paths) are stored locally on your PC in the app data folder. They are removed on uninstall.
  • Reports (CSV, PDF) are saved only where you choose.

Purchase and installation are handled by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement. We only receive aggregated sales and crash statistics without names.
